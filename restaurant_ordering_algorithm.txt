PRACTICAL 61 – RESTAURANT ORDERING SYSTEM
ALGORITHM

1. Start.

2. Display the title "RESTAURANT ORDERING SYSTEM".

3. Accept the customer name.

4. Initialize:
   - subtotal = 0
   - total_items = 0
   - quantity of every menu item = 0
   - amount of every ordered item = 0

5. Display the restaurant menu repeatedly:
      1. Veg Burger       Rs.120
      2. Veg Pizza        Rs.250
      3. Sandwich         Rs.150
      4. French Fries     Rs.100
      5. Cold Drink       Rs.60
      6. Generate Bill
      7. Exit

6. Accept the user's choice.

7. If the choice is 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5:
   a. Accept the quantity.
   b. Check that the quantity is greater than zero.
   c. Calculate item total = price × quantity.
   d. Add the item total to subtotal.
   e. Add the quantity to total_items.
   f. Store the quantity and item total for generating the bill later.
   g. Display that the item was added.
   h. Return to the menu.

8. If the choice is 6:
   a. Check whether at least one item has been ordered.
   b. If no item is ordered, display "No items ordered" and return to the menu.
   c. Otherwise calculate GST = subtotal × 5 / 100.
   d. Calculate grand_total = subtotal + GST.
   e. Display the restaurant bill containing:
      - Customer name
      - Ordered items
      - Quantity
      - Individual amount
      - Sub Total
      - GST (5%)
      - Grand Total
      - Total number of items
   f. Display "Thank You! Visit Again."
   g. Return to the menu.

9. If the choice is 7:
   a. Display an exit message.
   b. Stop the program.

10. If the choice is anything other than 1 to 7:
    Display "Invalid choice" and return to the menu.

11. End.
